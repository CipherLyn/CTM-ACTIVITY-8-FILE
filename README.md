# 🛡️ Cybersecurity Log Analysis Activity

## Activity Overview

In this activity, you will investigate three sets of system logs to identify and analyze different cybersecurity attacks. Each dataset is provided without revealing the actual attack type. Your task is to examine the evidence, identify the attack, determine its status, and explain the possible consequences if the attack successfully compromises its target.

## 📁 Activity Files

Download the following CSV files from the repository:

1. `Attack_1.csv`
2. `Attack_2.csv`
3. `Attack_3.csv`

Open the files using Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, or any compatible CSV viewer.

## 📝 Instructions

For EACH attack dataset, complete the following tasks:

**Task 1: Identify the Attack**

- Determine the type of cyberattack represented by the log entries.
- Explain the characteristics that helped you identify the attack.

**Task 2: Analyze the System Logs**

- Examine the timestamps, source IP addresses, destination IP addresses, event types, status, severity, and log details.
- Identify at least THREE (3) significant pieces of evidence.
- Explain the suspicious activities recorded in the logs.

**Task 3: Determine the Attack Status**

Classify the attack as:

- Successful
- Unsuccessful
- Partially Successful

Justify your answer using specific evidence from the CSV logs.

**Task 4: Explain the Successful Attack Scenario**

Assume that the attacker successfully executes the attack.

Explain:
- How the attack could affect the target system.
- What information, resources, or services could be compromised.
- The possible consequences for users and the organization.

**Task 5: Recommend Security Measures**

Provide at least THREE (3) appropriate security measures that can prevent, detect, or mitigate the identified attack.

## 📋 Required Output

Prepare a Cybersecurity Log Analysis Report containing the following sections:

1. Student Information
2. Attack 1 Analysis
3. Attack 2 Analysis
4. Attack 3 Analysis
5. Final Reflection

For each attack, include:

- Identified Attack Type
- Three Supporting Log Evidence Entries
- Attack Analysis and Explanation
- Attack Status and Justification
- Possible Scenario if Successful
- Prevention and Mitigation Recommendations

## 📤 Submission Guidelines

1. Complete your answers in a document.
2. Save your report in PDF format.

## ⚠️ Important Reminders

- Do not rely on the filename to identify the attack.
- Base your answers on actual evidence from the CSV files.
- Do not simply copy definitions from the internet.
- Explain your analysis using your own understanding.
- Recommendations must be relevant to the identified attack.
- All three attack datasets must be analyzed.

## 🎯 Learning Outcome

This activity aims to develop your ability to interpret security logs, recognize attack patterns, evaluate cybersecurity incidents, and recommend appropriate security controls.

**Remember:** A cybersecurity analyst does not simply identify an attack. A cybersecurity analyst investigates the evidence, evaluates the impact, and recommends appropriate countermeasures.
